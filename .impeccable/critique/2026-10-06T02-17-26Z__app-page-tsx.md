## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment: Category-interchangeable.** This design could belong to a hair salon, car wash, or yoga studio with a one-line copy swap. The amber-50→white gradient hero, four-card icon grid, three-testimonial row, and dark footer are the canonical Tailwind marketing template — executed cleanly, but authored for nobody in particular. PataFeliz has a declared 10-year neighborhood history, a São Paulo location, and an emotionally rich product (people's pets), none of which appear in the visual execution. No pet imagery. No geographic signal. No WhatsApp button despite it being São Paulo's dominant booking channel. The approved AI-generated visuals remain unused.

**Deterministic scan: 1 finding.** The detector flagged `app/globals.css` line 25 — Geist font identified as category-convergent (warning/slop severity). Not a false positive: Geist + Tailwind + amber-500 is an identifiable AI-template stack fingerprint.

## Overall Impression

The visual craft is above average for a first pass — amber discipline, section rhythm, and typography hierarchy are solid. The problem is that nothing about the execution is authored for this business in this city for these users. And at the conversion moment, the user clicks "Agendar Agora" and finds a phone number they can't tap. That's the whole game right there.

## What's Working

1. **Amber restraint is genuinely good.** The One Amber Rule is being followed implicitly — brand color appears in exactly the right four places.
2. **Section rhythm via background alternation.** White → amber-50 → white creates clean scroll cadence without dividers.
3. **Service clarity.** Four-card grid communicates the full offering in under five seconds.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Contact section delivers no booking mechanism**
- Phone `(11) 91234-5678` and email `contato@patafeliz.com.br` are plain text — no `tel:`, no `mailto:`, no WhatsApp button, no form.
- Fix: `<a href="tel:+5511912345678">`, `<a href="mailto:...">`, and a WhatsApp CTA as primary contact action.

**[P1] Mobile navigation completely absent**
- `hidden sm:flex` hides the entire nav below 640px with no hamburger/drawer replacement.
- Fix: Add hamburger toggle + mobile drawer, or bottom navigation strip.

**[P1] Page metadata still at Next.js scaffold defaults**
- `title: "Create Next App"`, `description: "Generated by create next app"`, `lang="en"` on Portuguese content.
- Fix: Real title, description, and `lang="pt-BR"`.

**[P1] No visible focus indicators**
- No `:focus-visible` styles anywhere. Tailwind preflight resets browser defaults.
- Fix: Add `focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-amber-500` to all interactive elements.

**[P2] Hero visual is an unstyled emoji placeholder**
- `<p className="text-5xl mb-6">🐶🐱🐰</p>` is the first visual the user sees.
- Fix: Replace with AI-generated hero image (approved per PRODUCT.md).

**[P2] Testimonials are uniform 5-star with interchangeable copy**
- Three identical 5-star cards with generic sentiment. Known trust anti-pattern.
- Fix: Vary star counts, add service specifics, differentiate emotional register.

## Minor Observations

- `lang="en"` on Portuguese page: WCAG 3.1.1 failure, screen reader mispronunciation
- 6+ emoji instances without `aria-hidden="true"` across page, header, footer
- Star ratings `{"★".repeat(t.stars)}` missing `aria-label="5 out of 5 stars"`
- No `<main>` element — screen reader users cannot skip to main content
- Header "Agendar" button ~30px tap height, below 44px minimum
- No Instagram link — primary local trust channel for São Paulo pet businesses
