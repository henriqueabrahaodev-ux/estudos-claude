# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript 5, TailwindCSS 4, Geist font via next/font

## Users

Pet owners in São Paulo, SP, arriving at the site to evaluate and book services for their dog or cat — grooming, vet visits, boarding, or shopping. Typically acting on a specific need (need a bath appointment, planning a trip) with moderate urgency and limited awareness of the business prior to landing.

## Product Purpose

PataFeliz is a local pet shop and veterinary center in São Paulo. The site's job is to convert a curious visitor into a booked appointment or walk-in. Success means the visitor understands what's available, trusts the business, and takes a booking action.

## Positioning

Demo / study project — no confirmed real differentiator established. Placeholder positioning: a full-service local pet center (grooming + veterinary + hotel + retail in one location) with 10+ years of neighborhood presence and a warm, personalized approach.

## Operating Context

Single-page marketing site (landing page). Visitors arrive via search, referral, or social. Primary conversion action: scheduling via the contact/booking anchor (#contato). Secondary: browsing services and building trust through differentials and testimonials.

## Brand Commitments

Name: PataFeliz. Logo mark: 🐾 paw emoji used as brand symbol. Tagline in use: "Cuidando com amor de quem você mais ama." Amber-orange as identity color.

## Evidence on Hand

All testimonials, photos, and social proof are currently placeholder. AI-generated visuals are approved for use in place of real photography. No real logo file, brand guide, or press on hand.

## Product Principles

1. **Trust before conversion** — visitors need confidence in the people, not just the services.
2. **Warmth without kitsch** — pet care is emotional; the design earns affection through quality, not just cute assets.
3. **Friction-free booking** — every section should funnel toward a clear, low-resistance next step.
4. **Local pride** — São Paulo neighborhood business; the site should feel present and personal, not generic.
5. **Completeness signals care** — showing grooming, vet, hotel, and shop together communicates a trustworthy full-service partner.
